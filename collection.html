Collection

the films watched and liked, and the filmmakers behind them

These pages are built from the films already seen and the filmmakers who matter most in them. The directors map and the network show how those filmmakers connect, and why. The circle places them by direction, from the canonical centre out to the experimental edge.

The isometric map and the specimen sheet read the same research in other forms. The watchlist is what comes next.

The pages

  • Directors — the directors map with its affinities, circles, and full list in two tiers
  • Network — the directors and the weighted connections between them
  • Directions — the collection's own circular diagram on five axes from the canonical centre outward
  • Isometric map — every director on the circle as a block placed by axis and era and raised by films seen
  • Specimen sheet — directors against techniques and concerns drawn as small ink marks
  • Watchlist — the films picked to watch next in order of year
