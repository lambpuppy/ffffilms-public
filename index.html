ilms

A navigable map of cinema. It connects filmmakers, films, and the concepts that run between them. Each entry says what it touches, and you find your way around by following those connections.

ffffilms is a conceptual map of cinema built from a single collection. It consists of 3,071 films seen and queued, and a directors map of 252 favourite filmmakers whose affinities are expressed by connections; 40 of them being core, while the rest make up the full list around them.

Alongside the map are the references it is read against. There is an entry-points guide, Schrader's diagram of the transcendental style, a transcribed list of discoveries, and a field guide to other critics. A gap analysis then compares the collection with all of them to show what is missing.

Where to start

  • Directors — the directors map with its affinities, circles, and full list in two tiers
  • Network — the directors and the weighted connections between them
  • Exploration — how the collection compares with the references it is read against
  • Numbers — the collection in numbers
  • Watchlist — the films picked to watch next in order of year
