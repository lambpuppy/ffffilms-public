ffffilms is a collection of visualisations and references built from my film-watching history. 253 favourite filmmakers have been analysed to show the influences, affinities, and movements between them, 40 of them at the core.

Alongside the visualisations are the references that help find new filmmakers to explore: an entry-points guide, Paul Schrader's diagram of the transcendental style, a transcribed list of discoveries by Borja Castillejo Calvo, and a field guide to other critics.
