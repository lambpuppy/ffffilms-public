ffffilms is a collection of visualisations and references built from my film-watching history. Favourite filmmakers have been analysed to show the influences, affinities, and movements between them.

Alongside the visualisations are the references that help find new filmmakers to explore: Paul Schrader's diagram of the transcendental style, a transcribed list of discoveries by Borja Castillejo Calvo, and a field guide to other critics.
