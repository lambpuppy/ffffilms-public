Exploration

84 filmmakers in the guide · 55 directors on the chart · 647 discoveries · counts live

Four references sit beside the map. The entry-points guide was written with this collection in mind. Schrader's diagram charts where arthouse cinema went after it left narrative. The other two are cinesinfin's discoveries of 2023 and Borja Castillejo Calvo's all-time canon. Each of them names films and filmmakers, and this page asks each one the same question. Where are the collection's gaps?

Every count, mark, and table here is worked out from films.json and directors.json each time the site is built. The reference pages use the same index for their marks, so the tables and counts stay up to date. A gap is a film or filmmaker that a reference names and the collection holds nothing of. A queued film counts as pending instead of as a gap.

The references

  • Entry points — 84 filmmakers in five clusters with two starting films each
  • Schrader's diagram — the transcendental style charted: three directions and the Tarkovsky Ring
  • Borja's canon — his all-time 463 transcribed and checked against the collection
  • Vitamin V — the 102 moving-image artists in Phaidon's 2025 survey with where to read about and watch each one
  • Topics — one subject at a time traced through its artists and works
  • Field guide — critics, blogs, and archives around the same territory with links to each

Against Schrader's diagram

The chart names 55 directors across its three directions, plus two schools: Italian Neorealism and New Romanian Cinema. The collection holds something by 51 of them. The Mandala's three directors are all present. Bresson is core with eleven films seen, and everything held by Ozu and Dreyer has already been seen. The same is true of Mizoguchi, Antonioni, and Lynch in the Art Gallery. Reading the whole chart also brings in names an earlier sample left out. Buñuel, Malick, Jarmusch, and Tarkovsky all sit inside the ring, which is where the collection sits too.

The thin places sit at the boundaries he drew. Sokurov is inside the ring but crossed off the map with two films. Wang Bing is past the ring and has one queued film and no node. Only four names on the whole chart are entirely unheld: Zhengfan Yang, Dietrich Brüggemann, Andy Warhol, and the unverified Ben Stamper. The collection sits inside the Tarkovsky Ring and leans toward the Mandala, which is where the diagram places the paying audience.

Dorsky is at the top with sixteen films and three held. Sokurov has fourteen and two held. These gaps point the same way as the other references. The experimental and silent poets the canon is built on are where the collection is thinnest.

Common ground across the references

Read together, the references agree on three gaps. The first is the structural film line. The guide's absent filmmakers sit on its Structural axis, and Beavers recurs through the discoveries lists. Borja's canon puts Dorsky at the top with sixteen films and three held. Past Schrader's ring the collection thins, and of the fifteen names outside it two are entirely unheld and six more have a single film. The second gap is the Mandala's long tail. It includes Oliveira's sixteen absent films and the devotional picks of core directors that are still waiting in the queue, such as Akerman's From the East and the Straubs' Too Early, Too Late. Everything held by Dreyer has already been watched. The third is Tricontinental, where twelve of the thirteen guide names are unwatched and the discoveries list's Kaul and Shahani picks match two held films between them.

Where they differ is in emphasis. Schrader's chart is nearly covered because the collection was in effect built inside his ring. The guide's gaps sit at its own edges instead of its centre. The discoveries list and Borja's canon are the places to look for films that fill any of these gaps. Most of what they name is absent from the collection, and both are full of the territory the other references say is missing.
