Directions

the collection's own circular diagram; the favourites worth placing and the whole entry-points guide, on the guide's five axes

This is a circular map in the spirit of Schrader's diagram, drawn from this collection instead of from his book. Five axes run from the centre to the edge. Each one stands for a cluster of the entry-points guide. Names near the centre are the more canonical filmmakers, and names further out are more experimental. Tier-1 favourites are labelled in bold. A few tier-2 favourites are also named where an axis needs one. The other tier-2 favourites and two founders from the guide appear as small dots, and their names show on hover and in the lists below. A handful of mainstream favourites are left off the chart altogether.

Each placement is a judgement, just as an edge is. The distance from the centre says how far from the canon this collection holds a filmmaker to be. On some lanes a filmmaker also leans to one side, and the side shows which neighbouring axis they are drawn toward. The reading lists below give every name on each axis from the centre outward. They also stand in for the diagram when it can't be drawn.
