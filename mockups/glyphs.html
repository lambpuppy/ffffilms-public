Director glyphs

One compact mark per director, built only from what the data already holds: axis, secondary axis, how canonical, tier, and how many edges of each kind. A shared vocabulary any view could reuse.

The vocabulary

Outer shape: the primary axis. Filled centre: how canonical the diagram holds the director to be, shrinking toward the experimental rim. Tick: a secondary axis, pointing where that axis lies on the diagram. Stroke weight: tier. Rules below, in the accent (it marks relations only): affinity, influence, collaboration, their length the count. A future inner mark would carry register once the traits data exists.
