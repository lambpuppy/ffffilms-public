This view tests an argument from VIEWS.md §2 against the collection. If the objection were simply to heightened emotion, Sirk and Fassbinder would sit beside the sentimental films they are said to resemble. They don't. What sets them apart is artifice you can see and a refusal to tell the viewer how to feel.
