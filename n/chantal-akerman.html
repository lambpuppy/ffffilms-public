Chantal Akerman

Film · Belgium · 1950–2015

In the network · 10 edges

Filmmaker and artist, born in Brussels. She made her first short, Saute ma ville, in 1968. In the early 1970s she worked in New York, where she saw the films of Michael Snow and Jonas Mekas. Her features include Je, tu, il, elle (1974), Jeanne Dielman, 23 quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles (1975), News from Home (1977), and Les Rendez-vous d'Anna (1978).

Her films often hold a fixed, frontal camera on a room, a corridor, or a street for a long take in close to real time. An everyday routine such as cooking or waiting becomes the subject. Women's lives, travel between cities, and her family's history as Polish Jews recur across the work. From the 1990s she also made installations that adapted her films for galleries. Her last film, No Home Movie (2015), was made with her mother, an Auschwitz survivor who is present in much of her work.
