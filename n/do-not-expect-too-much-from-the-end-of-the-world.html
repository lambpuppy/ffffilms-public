Do Not Expect Too Much
from the End of the World

Film · Romania / Croatia / France / Luxembourg · 2023 · 164 min

Angela (Ilinca Manolache), an overworked production assistant, drives around Bucharest casting a workplace-safety film. Between delays she interviews a worker injured through negligence and records rants to camera as an Andrew Tate-style alter ego.

Cut into her day is Angela merge mai departe, Lucian Bratu's 1981 film about a Bucharest taxi driver, so its Angela meets the new one across forty years.
