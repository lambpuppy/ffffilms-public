Macario

Film · Mexico · 1960 · 91 min

Macario (Ignacio López Tarso), a poor woodcutter, wants a whole turkey to himself. On the day he gets one, God, the Devil, and Death each ask to share it. He shares with Death, and Death grants him the power to heal on terms that finally take him.

It is based on a story by B. Traven and was photographed by Gabriel Figueroa. It was the first Mexican film nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.
