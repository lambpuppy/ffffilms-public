Sombre

Film · France · 1998 · 112 min

A man (Marc Barbé) drives through France in summer, and women die where he stops. A woman travelling alone (Elina Lowensohn) crosses his path and stays with him.

The image rarely rises above the dark. Faces come apart into grain, and the scenes are carried as much by the soundtrack of rain, breathing, and engine as by what can be seen. It received a special mention from the jury at Locarno in 1998.
