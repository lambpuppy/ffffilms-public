The Mother and
the Whore

Film · France · 1973 · 219 min

Alexandre (Jean-Pierre Léaud) lives off books and café tables in the flat of Marie (Bernadette Lafont), who runs a boutique. He begins an affair with Véronika (Françoise Lebrun), a nurse, and the three of them talk in cafés, on beds, and at kitchen tables for most of the film's 219 minutes.

It won the Grand Prix at Cannes in 1973. It was shot in black and white.
