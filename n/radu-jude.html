Radu
Jude

Film · Romania · 1977–

In the network · 4 edges

Filmmaker, born in Bucharest in 1977. The Happiest Girl in the World (2009) began a run of features. Aferim! (2015) won the Silver Bear for directing at Berlin, and Bad Luck Banging or Loony Porn (2021) won the Golden Bear. Between the features he makes found-footage essays from Romanian archives, such as The Dead Nation (2017) and The Marshal's Two Executions (2018).

His films are arguments about history and the images that carry it. I Do Not Care If We Go Down in History as Barbarians (2018) restages a 1941 massacre as a public re-enactment to see what it still hides. Do Not Expect Too Much from the End of the World (2023) runs a production assistant's working day against TikTok monologues and a 1981 Romanian feature. He mixes fixed-camera realism with title cards, archive footage, and internet material, and he often splits a film into sections in different styles.
