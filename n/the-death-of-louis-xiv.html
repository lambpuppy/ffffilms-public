Film

The Death
of Louis XIV

Film · France / Portugal / Spain · 2016 · 115 min

Louis XIV comes back from the hunt with a pain in his leg. The leg turns gangrenous, the court gathers around the bed, and the film keeps to the rooms and the pace of the dying. Jean-Pierre Léaud, in his seventies, plays the king in the wig and the robe.

It was photographed by candlelight and shot in fifteen days with no rehearsals, and the first day of shooting was Léaud's first day as the king. The duration is the diagnosis, as a body fails in something close to real time.
