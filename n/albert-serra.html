Albert
Serra

Film · Spain · 1975–

In the network · 3 edges

Filmmaker, born in Banyoles, Catalonia, in 1975. He studied literature and worked in theatre before film. His features use old texts as scaffolds: Honor de cavalleria (2006) from Cervantes, Birdsong (2008) from the Three Kings, and Story of My Death (2013), which crosses Casanova with Dracula. The Death of Louis XIV (2016) has Jean-Pierre Léaud dying by candlelight, and Liberté (2019), Pacifiction (2022), and the bullfighting documentary Afternoons of Solitude (2024) followed.

He shoots with several cameras running for hours while non-actors and a few professionals improvise in costume, then finds the film in the edit. The light is natural or candlelight, and the takes wait. His subjects are myths and powerful men at the moment they run down: a knight, a king, a libertine, a colonial official. The films move at the pace of the nights they were shot in and give the viewer no plot to hold on to, so boredom and decadence become the texture of the work.
