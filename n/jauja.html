Jauja

Film · Argentina / Denmark / France · 2014 · 110 min

In Patagonia in the 1880s, a Danish military engineer (Viggo Mortensen) travels with his teenage daughter. She disappears with a young soldier, and his search goes on past its object, through desert, a strange woman, and a dog. It was shot in the 4:3 ratio with rounded corners.

It won the FIPRESCI prize in the Un Certain Regard section at Cannes in 2014. The search dissolves into time. A dog leads Dinesen to a cave where an old woman speaks Danish and may be his daughter, and the film ends in present-day Denmark with a girl, a dog, and a toy soldier.
