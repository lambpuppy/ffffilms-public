Film

Jeanne
Dielman

Film · Belgium / France · 1975 · 201 min

The film covers three days in the flat of a widow who keeps house for her son and receives a male client each afternoon. Delphine Seyrig plays Jeanne. The camera is fixed, frontal, and at seated height, and the domestic tasks of peeling potatoes, making coffee, and washing dishes are shown in full.

It premiered at the Directors' Fortnight in Cannes in 1975. In 2022 it was voted first in the Sight and Sound critics' poll.
