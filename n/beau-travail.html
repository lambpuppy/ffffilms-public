Beau Travail

Film · France / Germany · 1999 · 93 min

A Foreign Legion unit in Djibouti drills, builds a road, irons, and goes to clubs at night. Sergeant Galoup (Denis Lavant) turns on Sentain (Grégoire Colin), and the narration, a letter written long after, circles the memory of what he did without explaining it. It is loosely based on Melville's Billy Budd.

Agnès Godard shot it. It ends with Galoup alone in a nightclub, dancing to Corona's 'The Rhythm of the Night', which is the release the whole film withholds.
