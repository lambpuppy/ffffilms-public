Film

Persona

Film · Sweden · 1966 · 85 min

An actress, Elisabet Vogler, stops speaking in the middle of a performance of Electra and is moved to a summer cottage with a nurse, Alma, who talks for her. Alma keeps talking as the boundary between the two women gives way. Liv Ullmann and Bibi Andersson play them.

It opens with a projector's arc light and a boy reaching toward a screen. In the middle the print burns and breaks, and the two faces fuse into one image. Sven Nykvist shot it.

Connections

  • directed by Ingmar Bergman Persona has two women, one island cottage, and a silence that changes hands. It distils Bergman's chamber style.
