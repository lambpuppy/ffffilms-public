The Green Ray

Film · France · 1986 · 99 min

Delphine is on a holiday she cannot settle into. A trip to Greece falls through, she leaves a weekend in Cherbourg early, and she tries the Alps and gives up. She drifts between Paris and Biarritz alone and hears strangers talk about Jules Verne's novel of the same name, whose promised flash of green at sunset no one in the film has seen. Marie Rivière plays her.

It was built with the cast, who invented their own dialogue, and shot in order on 16 mm to stay inconspicuous. The production's only major expense was the trip to the Canary Islands to film the green ray for real. It won the Golden Lion at Venice in 1986.
