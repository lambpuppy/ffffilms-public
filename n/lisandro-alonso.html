Lisandro
Alonso

Film · Argentina · 1975–

In the network · 1 edge

Filmmaker, born in Buenos Aires in 1975. He worked as an assistant and camera operator before La libertad (2001). Los Muertos (2004), Fantasma (2006), Liverpool (2008), Jauja (2014), and Eureka (2023) followed. Jauja, with Viggo Mortensen, moved his method into 1880s Patagonia and won the FIPRESCI prize in the Un Certain Regard section at Cannes.

The early films are built from long static takes, natural sound, and non-professional actors. Dialogue is scarce, and the plots are journeys from one place to another: a woodcutter's day, a man released from prison travelling upriver, a sailor returning home. The real time of work and travel is left intact, so the films stay close to documentary. From Jauja on, dream logic and leaps across time and place break into that patience, and Eureka moves between a western, a Native American reservation, and the Amazon.
