Lois
Patiño

Film · Spain · 1983–

In the network · 2 edges

Filmmaker, born in Vigo in 1983, working mostly on the Galician coast. His early short films are studies of landscape held in time, and their titles say so directly: Duration-Landscape: Rocks (2011) and Duration-Landscape: Road (2012). Costa da Morte (2013) and Night Without Distance (2015) followed, then Red Moon Tide (2020) and Samsara (2023).

He works with a fixed camera and long, wide shots in which figures stand small in the terrain and the light does the moving. People are often frozen like statues while the sea, the wind, and the weather keep going, and voices are whispered close to the microphone while the bodies stay far away. His later films bring in ritual and the dead. Red Moon Tide is set on the Galician coast, and Samsara follows a soul from Laos to Zanzibar, with a long passage of flickering light that the audience watches with their eyes closed.
