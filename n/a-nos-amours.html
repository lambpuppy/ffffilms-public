A Nos
Amours

Film · France · 1983 · 102 min

Suzanne (Sandrine Bonnaire, in her first screen role) moves through boyfriends while her family comes apart around her. Her father, played by Pialat himself, walks out, and her mother holds the household to its forms.

It was co-written with Arlette Langmann and drawn in part from her own adolescence.
