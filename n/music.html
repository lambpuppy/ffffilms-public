Music

Film · Germany / France / Greece / Serbia · 2023 · 109 min

A boy left with a Greek couple as an infant grows up there. As a man in Berlin he comes within reach of where he came from, in a retelling of the Oedipus story with the recognition withheld. The scenes arrive as fragments of a mother, a wound, and a reunion, connected by what the audience knows more than by what the film tells.

It won the Silver Bear for screenplay at Berlin in 2023. What Sophocles staged as revelation the film keeps as half-knowledge, and the characters walk past their myth without being told its name.
