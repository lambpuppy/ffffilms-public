Mirror

Film · Soviet Union · 1975 · 107 min

The film is an autobiographical assembly of the narrator's wartime childhood, his mother at a printing press, his father's poems read over the images, and newsreel of a stratospheric balloon and soldiers crossing Lake Sivash. Margarita Terekhova plays both the mother and the wife, faces recur across generations, and the dying narrator is never shown.

The order follows association instead of chronology, with dreams, memories, and documentary interleaved, and the film was recut many times before release. Soviet authorities gave it limited distribution, but its reputation travelled anyway.
