Philippe
Grandrieux

Film · France · 1954–

In the network · 4 edges

Filmmaker, born in Saint-Étienne in 1954. He studied film at INSAS in Belgium, and in the 1980s he worked with the INA and La Sept/Arte on experimental television. His features are Sombre (1998), La Vie nouvelle (2002), Un lac (2008), and Malgré la nuit (2015). Meurtrière (2015), a shorter film made with dancers, condenses his method.

His films work at the edge of what can be seen. The camera is handheld and stays close to bodies, the image is often barely above darkness or out of focus, and the soundtrack carries as much of the scene as the picture does. The stories are simple and violent, such as a killer on the road in Sombre, and they are told through sensation more than explanation. He often operates the camera himself, moving with the actors as if dancing with them.
