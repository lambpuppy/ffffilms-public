Film

Floating
Weeds

Film · Japan · 1959 · 115 min

A troupe of travelling actors arrives by boat at a seaside town. Their master (Ganjirō Nakamura) has a son there (Hiroshi Kawaguchi) who has been told his father is his uncle. The master's mistress (Machiko Kyō) hires a young actress (Ayako Wakao) to seduce the son as revenge, and the two fall in love.

It is a remake of Ozu's own silent A Story of Floating Weeds (1934). It was planned first for Shochiku and then made as his only film for Daiei, with Kazuo Miyagawa as cinematographer.
