Éric
Rohmer

Film · France · 1920–2010

In the network · 5 edges

Filmmaker and critic, born Maurice Schérer in 1920. He taught literature in schools and belonged to the Cahiers du cinéma circle from its start, editing the magazine from 1957 to 1963. The Six Moral Tales, including My Night at Maud's (1969), made his name, and the Comedies and Proverbs and the Tales of the Four Seasons followed across four decades. He died in 2010.

His films are made of conversation. His characters talk their way toward positions they then fail to hold, usually about love, and the talk carries the drama without feeling like theatre. He worked with small crews, natural light, and real locations, often by the sea or in the countryside in a particular season, and the cinematographer Néstor Almendros shot many of the films. The Green Ray (1986) took the Golden Lion at Venice with a structure built from an unscripted summer.
