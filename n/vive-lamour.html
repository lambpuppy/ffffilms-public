Vive
L'Amour

Film · Taiwan · 1994 · 118 min

Three people pass through an empty Taipei apartment held for sale: the estate agent with the key, a young woman selling clothes on the street, and a young man with nowhere else to go. None of them quite meet.

It won the Golden Lion at Venice in 1994. The last shot holds the estate agent crying on a park bench for minutes without a cut.
