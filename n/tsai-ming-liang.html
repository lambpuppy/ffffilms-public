Tsai
Ming-liang

Film · Taiwan / Malaysia · 1957–

In the network · 3 edges

Filmmaker, born in Kuching, Malaysia, in 1957. He moved to Taiwan in his twenties and studied theatre in Taipei. His features run from Rebels of the Neon God (1992) to Days (2020), and Lee Kang-sheng appears in all of them. Vive L'Amour won the Golden Lion at Venice in 1994.

His films keep a small set of elements: Taipei apartments, water in leaks and floods, characters who barely speak, and shots held long after the event. Loneliness and desire are his subjects, often told through the body, as Lee Kang-sheng ages across three decades of films. Musical numbers sometimes break into the silence, as in The Hole (1998) and The Wayward Cloud (2005). From Visage (2009) he has also made shorter films and installations, including the Walker series, in which a monk walks through cities at a barely visible pace.
