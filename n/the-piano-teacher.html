The Piano
Teacher

Film · France / Austria / Germany · 2001 · 131 min

Erika (Isabelle Huppert) teaches piano at a Vienna conservatory and lives with her mother (Annie Girardot). A student (Benoît Magimel) pursues her, and the pursuit runs into the terms of her fantasies. It is adapted from Elfriede Jelinek's novel.

At Cannes in 2001 the film won the Grand Prix and both acting prizes.
