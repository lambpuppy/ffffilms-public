Tide

Film · Spain · 2020 · 84 min

On the Costa da Morte the villagers stand motionless while their voices speak of ghosts, witches, and monsters. Three women arrive to find Rubio, a diver lost at sea.

It premiered at the Berlinale in 2020. The colour moves toward red as the search closes in, so the light of the title arrives in the grade.
