Film

Woman in
the Dunes

Film · Japan · 1964 · 147 min

An entomologist on a collecting trip misses the last bus back, and the villagers of the dunes put him up in a house at the bottom of a sand pit with a young widow. The house must be dug out each night or it will be buried, and the village lives by selling the sand. He plans escapes, and the sand keeps its own time.

It is adapted from Kōbō Abe's novel, with Abe's screenplay and Tōru Takemitsu's score. It won the Jury Prize at Cannes in 1964 and an Academy Award nomination for Teshigahara as director.
