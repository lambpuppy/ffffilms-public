Bruno
Dumont

Film · France · 1958–

In the network · 4 edges

Filmmaker, born in Bailleul in the Nord in 1958. He studied and taught philosophy before La Vie de Jésus (1997), his first feature. Humanité (1999), Flanders (2006), Hadewijch (2009), Camille Claudel 1915 (2013), and Slack Bay (2016) followed, along with two films on Joan of Arc, Jeannette (2017) and Joan (2019).

He casts non-actors from the Nord and films them in wide, flat landscapes where long stretches of stillness give way to sudden violence or sex. His subjects are grace, evil, and the body, treated without explanation. For Humanité its two non-professional leads won both acting prizes at Cannes, and the film took the Grand Prix. From Li'l Quinquin (2014) on, his work alternates this austerity with farce, and The Empire (2023) sets the same coastal villages inside a science-fiction war between good and evil.
