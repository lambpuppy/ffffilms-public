Angela
Schanelec

Film · Germany · 1962–

In the network · 5 edges

Filmmaker and actress, born in Germany in 1962. She acted in theatre and film through the 1980s and then studied at the Berlin film academy DFFB. Her features include Passing Summer (2001), Marseille (2004), Orly (2010), The Dreamed Path (2016), I Was at Home, But… (2019), which won the Silver Bear for direction at Berlin, and Music (2023).

She is a central figure of the Berlin School. Her films withhold causes, so deaths and meetings happen offscreen or between scenes, and the viewer meets only their consequences. The framing is frontal and exact, the actors speak with a flat delivery, and gestures are filmed with a Bresson-like attention to hands and bodies. She also retells classical material at a distance. Music runs the Oedipus story as a contemporary family's half-heard history, and I Was at Home, But… sets a boy's disappearance beside schoolchildren performing Hamlet.
