Film

Sans
Soleil

Film · France · 1983 · 100 min

A woman reads letters from a travelling cameraman, the fictional Sandor Krasna, over his images from Japan, Guinea-Bissau, Iceland, and San Francisco. The letters observe memory, ritual, and images themselves: a video arcade, a zoo, and the faces of passengers dozing on a ferry.

One detour walks through a derelict quarter of Tokyo that the letters call 'the Zone', after Tarkovsky's Stalker, giving a real city the fiction's name.
