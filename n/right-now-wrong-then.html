Right Now,
Wrong Then

Film · South Korea · 2015 · 121 min

A film director, Ham Cheon-su, arrives in Suwon to give a talk and spends a day with Yoon Hee-jeong, a young painter. They visit a temple and a café and have dinner with her friends over too much soju. The film then plays the day again with the same places and people and a different honesty. Kim Joo-hyuk and Kim Min-hee star.

It won the Golden Leopard at Locarno in 2015. The two passes are the same in outline, and the film argues that the difference in how people behave is the whole of the difference.
