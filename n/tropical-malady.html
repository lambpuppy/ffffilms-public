Tropical
Malady

Film · Thailand / France · 2004 · 118 min

A soldier, Keng, courts Tong, a country boy in a small Isan town. At the midpoint the story restarts as a folk tale, with the same two figures as a hunter and a tiger spirit in a jungle at night, guided by a talking monkey. Banlop Lomnoi and Sakda Kaewbuadee star.

It won the Jury Prize at Cannes in 2004. The two halves share a logic more than a plot: desire in daylight, and the fear of the same desire by night.
