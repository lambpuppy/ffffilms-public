Ali: Fear Eats
the Soul

Film · Germany · 1974 · 93 min

Emmi, a widowed cleaning woman in her sixties, meets Ali, a Moroccan guest worker, in a bar where the foreign workers drink after work. They marry, and the disapproval arrives as neighbourly concern. Brigitte Mira and El Hedi ben Salem play the couple.

It is remade from Douglas Sirk's All That Heaven Allows (1955). The plot is kept and every social fact is changed: the American small town becomes Munich's stairwells, and the pressure arrives as politeness.

Connections

  • directed by Rainer Werner Fassbinder Fassbinder remade All That Heaven Allows in Munich around a Moroccan guest worker and a cleaning woman. He made it between bigger projects, and it outlasted them.
