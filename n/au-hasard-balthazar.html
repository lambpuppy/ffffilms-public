Au hasard
Balthazar

Film · France / Sweden · 1966 · 95 min

The film follows the life of a donkey, from farm pet through circus, wasteland, and coal delivery, as he passes from hand to hand. Marie, the farmer's daughter who loved him as a foal, crosses his path throughout. The performers are non-professionals, and Schubert's A major Andantino returns over the images.

Schrader's Transcendental Style in Film (1972) takes Bresson, with Ozu and Dreyer, as its three directors. Its thesis is that the everyday can be raised to a final stillness.
