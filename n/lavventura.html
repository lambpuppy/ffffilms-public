L'Avventura

Film · Italy / France · 1960 · 143 min

A group of friends on a sailing trip stop at a bare volcanic island, and Anna disappears. The search for her drives the film. Sandro, her lover, and Claudia, her friend, take it up, and it quietly gives way to their own affair. Monica Vitti, Gabriele Ferzetti, and Lea Massari star.

It premiered at Cannes in 1960, was booed at its first screening, and won the Jury Prize. The ending, on a hotel terrace, holds a two-shot that answers nothing about where Anna went.

Connections

  • directed by Michelangelo Antonioni L'Avventura was booed at Cannes in 1960 and then given the Jury Prize. It was the first of Antonioni's four films with Monica Vitti, and that run moved him from domestic melodrama to drift.
