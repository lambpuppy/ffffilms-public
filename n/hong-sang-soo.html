Hong
Sang-soo

Film · South Korea · 1960–

In the network · 4 edges

Filmmaker, born in Seoul in 1960. He studied film in the United States, in California and then Chicago, before The Day a Pig Fell into the Well (1996) began a run of some thirty features in under three decades. Right Now, Wrong Then (2015) won the Golden Leopard at Locarno.

His films run on doubled scenes, retold days, and long meals with soju, and a sudden zoom marks each turn. His characters, often film directors and the people around them, drink, flirt, and embarrass themselves in conversations that circle back on each other. He writes quickly, often on the morning of the shoot, and makes most films in a few weeks on small budgets with a small company of actors, with Kim Min-hee at its centre since 2015. In recent years he has also shot, edited, and scored the films himself.
