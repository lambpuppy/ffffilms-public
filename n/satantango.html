Satantango

Film · Hungary / Germany / Switzerland · 1994 · 432 min

It is adapted from László Krasznahorkai's 1985 novel. A decaying collective farm and its remaining villagers wait for Irimiás, believed dead, who returns as a prophet. A doctor who never leaves his room writes it all down, a young girl's small cruelties run through the middle, and the rain continues.

The film has twelve chapters that run forward and back, like the steps of the tango in its title. It is in black and white, in long takes that walk with the characters, and runs over seven hours.
