Maurice
Pialat

Film · France · 1925–2003

In the network · 4 edges

Filmmaker, born in Cunlhat in 1925 and raised in a Paris suburb. He painted and acted before cinema and was past forty when his first feature, Naked Childhood, appeared in 1968. We Won't Grow Old Together (1972), The Mouth Agape (1974), Loulou (1980), and A Nos Amours (1983) followed. Under Satan's Sun (1987) won the Palme d'Or at a Cannes screening where he was booed, and Van Gogh (1991) was his last film. He died in 2003.

His films film private life at close range and at volume: couples breaking up, a mother dying, a teenage girl and her family at war. Scenes start late and are cut off before they resolve, and the gaps between them are left unexplained. He provoked his actors on set, sometimes changing a scene without warning, to get real hostility and surprise into the performances. The result feels close to documentary, although every scene is written and staged.
