critics, blogs and archives around this territory

These are other critics, blogs, and archives that cover the same territory as cinesinfin. Some follow the slow and contemplative line, and others the avant-garde and underground. Every site below was checked in September 2026. The blurbs describe what was found there, and the links go to the source.

Around cinesinfin

  • cinesinfin — Borja Castillejo Calvo — Essays, interviews, and annual discoveries lists by one critic whose writing covers much of this collection's territory, including Sokurov, Tarkovsky, Barley, and Kelemen. The discoveries of 2023 are transcribed on this site.
  • Revista Mutaciones — A Spanish online film magazine with new releases, festivals, interviews, and a numbered historical archive. Castillejo writes here on Sokurov's romanticism, Aleksei German Jr., Yufit's necrorealism, Loznitsa, and Geyrhalter.
  • Cine Transit — “Transit: cine y otros desvíos” publishes Spanish-language criticism, interviews, and video essays. It has published Castillejo's pieces on Scott Barley, Dean Kavanagh, Eric Pauwels, and Phil Solomon's ROM trilogy.

Scholars

  • Erika Balsom — A scholar and critic of film and video art, based in London and teaching at King's College London. Her Ten Skies (Fireflies Press, 2021) is a book-length reading of James Benning's film of ten fixed shots of the sky. Her other books include After Uniqueness (2017), on how film and video art circulate, and An Oceanic Feeling (2018), on cinema and the sea. She also wrote the introduction to Vitamin V. Also: an excerpt from Ten Skies, MUBI Notebook (2021).

Slow & contemplative

  • The Arts of (Slow) Cinema — Nadin Mai — Thirteen years of writing almost entirely on slow cinema, with reviews, interviews, a bibliography, and an A-to-Z index of filmmakers. The blog closed in July 2025, and the archive is still online.
  • Unspoken Cinema — The Contemporary Contemplative Cinema (CCC) blog has run since 2006 and is still posting. Its Index, Library, and Recommendations pages are organised ways into the slow-cinema canon.
  • Girish Shambu — A long-running cinephile blog on global and contemplative cinema, with annual lists of favourites and the book The New Cinephilia. It ranges more widely than the other sites here.

Avant-garde & underground

  • DC's — Dennis Cooper — Dennis Cooper's long-running blog covers obscure and underground film. Its year-end lists are built from readers' own lists and regularly turn up films this collection hasn't found yet.
  • Screen Slate — Screen Slate has published daily screening listings and articles for New York and San Francisco since 2011. Its record of past programmes also works as a map of the American avant-garde.
  • Light Industry — A New York screening room for artists' film and video. Its calendar, publications, and archive of past programmes add up to a course in the avant-garde and its edges.

Archives & distributors

  • Light Cone — A Paris distributor of experimental film since 1982. Its online catalogue has filmmaker pages and critical notes, and it is the source of the Mujer de Milfuegos record used on this site.
  • Re:Voir — A Paris distributor and streaming platform for the historical and contemporary avant-garde. R. Bruce Elder's The Book of Praise streams there in full.

The other route is to follow the guests. The contributors to the discoveries lists each publish writing and lists of their own: Dean Kavanagh, Antoni Orlof, Maximilien Luc Proctor, Park Kyujae, Elena Duque, Stefano Miraglia, Gabriel Linhares Falcão, and Kurt Walker. Their tastes are already known to overlap with this collection's. Every blurb above was written after checking the site in September 2026, and nothing is claimed that couldn't be seen on the live page.
