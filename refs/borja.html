Borja's canon

463 films · 143 held (97 seen, 46 queued) · marks resolved from the collection

Borja Castillejo Calvo kept an all-time canon on Letterboxd, and this page transcribes it in full. The list runs by year from newest to oldest and then alphabetically within each year. So the position numbers only mark where a film falls in the list and say nothing about preference. Directors are taken from the film pages themselves.

Of the 463 films, 143 are in this collection: 97 seen and 46 queued. The canon barely overlaps his own section of the 2023 discoveries list. The two record different things: what he holds as canon, and what he first watched that year. The recurring directors give the list its shape. Dorsky, Sokurov, and Epstein are at the top, well ahead of the names a standard canon would put there.
